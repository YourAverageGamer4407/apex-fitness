Install on your Android

This app is a PWA — it installs like a native app, works offline, and keeps your data on the phone.
  1. Host the folder (this file + manifest.json + sw.js) somewhere your phone can reach — the easiest free option is GitHub Pages, or any static host. Opening the raw file:// won't install.
  2. Open the hosted URL in Chrome on your Android.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen → Install.
  4. On your computer, use Export to download your data, then Import it on the phone once (data is stored per-device).
